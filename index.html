Before the Paper Curtain

A lightweight interactive stage story shaped by four rehearsal decisions.

Privacy Policy

Step into the role of Su Mo, a substitute stage manager for a community paper puppet festival. Before the 3:00 PM curtain, you must turn competing ideas into a clear opening performance.

Make four meaningful choices, place stage marks, unlock cue ribbons, and guide puppets and rhythm toward one of two complete endings.

Gameplay Screenshots

What You Will Do

  • Coordinate paper puppets, live rhythm, masks, and final staging cues.
  • Use simple interactions designed for a focused 12-18 minute story session.
  • Discover two full endings without failure states or online requirements.
