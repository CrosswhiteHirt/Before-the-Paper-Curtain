Overview

Before the Paper Curtain is designed as an offline interactive story game. We respect your privacy and keep the experience simple.

Data Collection

The app does not require account registration and does not ask users to submit personal information. Gameplay progress, settings, and ending unlocks may be stored locally on your device to support continue, restart, sound, and archive features.

Network and Sharing

The core game can be played without an online account. We do not sell personal data, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
